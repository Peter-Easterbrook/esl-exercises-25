💡 Tips for Release Notes

1. Keep it under 500 words - Users won't read long notes
2. Use bullet points - Easier to scan than paragraphs
3. Highlight privacy - Builds trust with new users
4. Include contact info - Shows you're approachable
5. Update for each release - Keep users informed of improvements
